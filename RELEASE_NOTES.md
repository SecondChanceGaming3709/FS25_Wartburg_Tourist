# FS25 Wartburg 353 Tourist — Version 1.0.6.0

## Release Summary

This release brings the classic Wartburg 353 Tourist to Farming Simulator 25 together with a matching functional camper trailer.

## Features

### Wartburg 353 Tourist

- 70 hp engine and four-speed manual transmission
- Approximately 105 km/h maximum speed
- Driver plus four passenger seats
- Functional lights, indicators, brake lights, dashboard gauges, and license plates
- Camper-compatible rear hitch
- Tension belts and opening tailgate
- Interactive interior controls
- Customizable body and seat colors
- New and rusted condition configurations
- Dirt and wear support

### Wartburg Tourist Camper

- Deployable camper and support
- Interactive opening camper door
- Functional sleeping point with a dedicated sleep camera
- Car, standard trailer, and low trailer hitch compatibility
- Functional road lights and reflectors
- Customizable body, fabric, and mattress colors
- New and rusted condition configurations
- Dirt and wear support

## Installation

Download `FS25_wartburg_tourist.zip`, leave the archive zipped, and place it in:

`Documents/My Games/FarmingSimulator2025/mods`

Enable the mod when loading your save.
